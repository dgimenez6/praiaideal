Trails and walks

One of Búzios’ highest viewpoints, between Tucuns and José Gonçalves. The Tucuns ascent is steeper; the alternative is longer. Plan to return in daylight.

Distance and time

Via Tucuns: about 1–1h45 return. Via José Gonçalves: about 2–2h45. Published reference from 2023.

Access and facilities

Bring water, grippy footwear and sun protection. Download the route, confirm access and arrange a daylight return. Avoid trails during storms and wet rocks or rocks reached by waves.

Confirm final access, parking, toilets, shade, prices and availability locally. The map is a reference location, not a hiking route.
