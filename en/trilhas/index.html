Trails and walks

Plan your day · PraiaIdeal

Ponta do Pai Vitório

Viewpoint in Rasa. The viewpoint walk and beach descent are different routes. Slopes are present; do not assume step-free access.

Serra das Emerências

One of Búzios’ highest viewpoints, between Tucuns and José Gonçalves. The Tucuns ascent is steeper; the alternative is longer. Plan to return in daylight.

Mangue de Pedras

Mangrove in Rasa. Visit separately or combine with Pai Vitório; the combined circuit is longer. Protect roots, wildlife and vegetation.

Caravelas x Pontal do Peró

Crossing from Caravelas via Amendoeira towards Pontal do Peró in Cabo Frio, through woodland and coastal sections. Arrange return transport and consider a local guide.

Ponta da Lagoinha

Walk to a geological coastal attraction. Duration depends on stops. Wet rocks can be slippery; keep clear of waves.

Tartaruga x Praia do Canto

Coastal crossing between Tartaruga and Canto, with variants via Virgens and Amores. Confirm the route and return plan. Rocky sections depend on sea conditions; consider a guide.

Praia Olho de Boi

Access from the right-hand end of Brava, up and over the hill. A naturist destination: check and respect local rules. Do not photograph others without permission.
